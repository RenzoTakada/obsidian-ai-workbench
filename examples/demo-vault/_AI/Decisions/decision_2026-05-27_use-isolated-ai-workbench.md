# Decision — Use an isolated AI workbench

Date: 2026-05-27
Status: accepted

## Context

AI-generated notes can pollute personal notes if there is no boundary.

## Decision

Keep AI-generated drafts, memory, sessions, specs, logs, and maintenance files inside `_AI/`.

## Consequences

- Human notes stay clean.
- AI output is easier to audit.
- Promotion into permanent notes stays a human decision.
