# Human vs AI Boundaries

The boundary is the most important part of the system.

## Recommended rule

```text
Inside _AI/:
  AI can create, edit, organize, and maintain files.

Outside _AI/:
  AI can only read or modify files when explicitly authorized by the human.
```

## Why

If an AI writes directly into your permanent notes, your second brain becomes less trustworthy. You may end up with notes you never processed, never understood, and never chose.

The value of a second brain comes from:

1. contact with information;
2. thinking about it;
3. writing in your own words;
4. connecting it with what you already know.

AI can assist that process, but should not replace it.

## Good AI behavior

The AI can:

- search notes;
- suggest links;
- summarize sources into drafts;
- compare new material with prior notes;
- create checklists;
- propose structure;
- maintain its own logs and outputs.

The AI should not:

- pretend its output is your permanent knowledge;
- silently edit human-authored notes;
- overwrite your thoughts;
- decide your thesis/angle for you;
- delete files without explicit approval.
