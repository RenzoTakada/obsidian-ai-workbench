# Maintenance

A second brain does not improve automatically just because an AI can write files.

You need maintenance.

## Weekly review

Review:

- `_AI/Outputs/`
- `_AI/Logs/`
- `_AI/Sessions/`
- `_AI/Memory/`
- `_AI/Decisions/`

Ask:

- What is still useful?
- What should be deleted or archived?
- What should become durable AI memory?
- What should the human rewrite as a permanent note?

## Promote carefully

AI output should become human knowledge only after the human reviews and rewrites it.

## Prune aggressively

More files do not mean more intelligence. Unmaintained notes become noise.

## Keep secrets out

Do not store credentials, tokens, private keys, or sensitive personal data in the AI workbench unless you know exactly what you are doing.
